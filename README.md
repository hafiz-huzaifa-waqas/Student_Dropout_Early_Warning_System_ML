🎓 Student Dropout Early Warning System (EWS)

FEMHACK 26:

Project Overview

This is the final project done in our AI & Data Science Hackathon held at Saylani Mass IT Training.

The system is designed to help universities predict which students are at risk of dropping out early in the semester.

It uses students’ academic activity and basic demographic information to calculate a risk score and classify students as Low, Medium, or High risk. Advisors can then focus on students who need attention before it’s too late.

This project combines data preprocessing, feature engineering, machine learning, and a user-friendly Streamlit app, providing a real-world solution for early intervention in education.

Dataset:

The dataset is from Kaggle:  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aljarah/xAPI-Edu-Data


Key Features
    ~  Predicts student dropout risk
    ~ Calculates risk score and risk label (Low / Medium / High)
    ~  Shows Top 20 high-risk students for advisors
    ~  Displays individual student details and top reasons for risk
    ~  Allows download of predictions CSV
    ~  Built with Python, Pandas, XGBoost, and Streamlit


Requirements: 
    Data set , python, streamlit , and libraries mentioned.
